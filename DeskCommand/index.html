What is DeskCommand?

DeskCommand turns your iPhone or iPad into a powerful remote for your Mac. Instead of getting up to your desk, trigger apps, run Shortcuts, and manage multiple Macs right from the palm of your hand.

What it does

Control Mac Apps

Launch, switch, and control applications on your Mac from your iPhone or iPad.

Run Shortcuts

Trigger your Mac's Shortcuts directly from your handheld device — even complex multi-step workflows.

Multiple Phones per Mac

A single Mac can be paired with several iPhones and iPads, letting everyone in the room drive the same machine.

Multiple Macs per Device

Pair one iPhone or iPad with several Mac hosts and switch between them instantly.

Simultaneous Control

Stay connected to multiple Macs at the same time and control all of them without disconnecting.

Secure Pairing

Device-to-device communication keeps your commands private between your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

How to install

DeskCommand is made of two free companion apps: one for your Mac host and one for your iPhone or iPad.

1

Install DeskCommand on your Mac

Download the Mac app via TestFlight. This runs on your computer and acts as the host for your phone and tablet.

2

Install DeskCommand on your iPhone or iPad

Download the iOS companion app via TestFlight. It becomes your remote control.

3

Pair your devices

Open both apps and follow the simple pairing flow. Keep your device and Mac on the same network, then approve the connection.

4

Control, repeat for more devices

Start launching apps, running Shortcuts, and switching between Macs. Repeat pairing for additional phones or more Mac hosts.
