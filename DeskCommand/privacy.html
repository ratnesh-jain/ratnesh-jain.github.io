Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DeskCommand ("we", "us") handles information when you use the DeskCommand Mac app and the DeskCommand iPhone and iPad companion apps.

Information we collect

DeskCommand is designed to be privacy-first. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any server operated by us. The connection between your devices is direct and device-to-device.

Data stored on your device

The apps store minimal data locally on your device to remember your paired connections. This may include a list of paired devices and their settings. This information never leaves your devices.

Communications between devices

When you control a Mac from your iPhone or iPad, commands such as launching an app or running a Shortcut are transmitted directly between your paired devices over your local network. These communications are not collected, logged, or transmitted by us.

Third-party services

DeskCommand does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services. We do not share your data with any third party.

Children's privacy

Our apps are general-purpose and not aimed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at ratneshjain1993@gmail.com.
